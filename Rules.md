# BlackJack 🂫
Classic Game Mode -- curent version of the game.

## Gameplay

Two cards are dealt to each player. The player chooses to stand (stop) whenever they want or hit (draw another card) as long as their score is under 21 (included).The dealar has two cards but one is face down and is only revealed at the end of the players'turn.

> Format - Players vs Dealer.
> Interaction - Individual scoring (PvE) with no direct interaction between players.

## Goal 

Get a BlackJack (Ace 🂡 + 10 🂪/ Jack 🂫/ Queen 🂭/ King 🂮) or get aas close to 21 as possible without exceeding it (which results in an instant loss/bust) to beat the dealer.

## Dealers Rules

The dealer must continue to draw cards until reaching a minimum score of 17.

## Betting and Scoring

Bets are placed every round.
Standard Win: If the player wins against the dealer (beats the dealer's scorewithout exceeding 21, or has a valid score while the dealer busts), the payout is 1:1 (wins the equivalent of their bet).
Blackjack: Payout is 1.5:1 (1.5 times the bet).
Push (Tie): The player gets their original bet back.
